# Aerofit Business Case – Descriptive Statistics & Probability

## Project Overview
This project analyzes customer characteristics for different Aerofit treadmill products using descriptive statistics and probability analysis. The goal is to understand customer behavior and help the company recommend suitable treadmills to new customers.

## Business Objectives
- Understand customer characteristics across Aerofit treadmill products.
- Compare customer demographics, income, fitness levels, and usage patterns.
- Analyze product preferences using probability and conditional probability.
- Develop customer profiles and business recommendations.

## Dataset Information
The dataset contains customer purchase information for Aerofit treadmills, including demographic, income, and fitness-related variables.

- No missing values were observed in the analysis.
- Categorical columns were converted to category data types.

## Key Findings
- KP281 has the highest number of purchases and is the most popular entry-level treadmill.
- Most customers fall between 20 and 35 years old.
- Customer income distribution is right-skewed.
- KP781 buyers generally have higher income, fitness levels, and usage frequency.
- Fitness and miles show a strong positive correlation.
- Male customers show a higher probability of purchasing KP781 compared with female customers.
- Partnered customers show slightly higher preference for mid-range and premium treadmills.

## Customer Segmentation

| Product | Target Customer | Key Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| KP281 | Beginners | Young, lower income, moderate usage |
| KP481 | Regular users | Middle income, consistent workouts |
| KP781 | Advanced users | Higher income, high fitness, heavy usage |

## Business Recommendations
1. **KP281 – Beginner Segment:** Market as an affordable, beginner-friendly treadmill.
2. **KP481 – Regular Users:** Promote as a value-for-money upgrade for consistent fitness users.
3. **KP781 – Premium Segment:** Target high-income customers and serious fitness enthusiasts.
4. **Personalized Marketing:** Recommend products based on income, fitness level, and usage patterns.
5. **Customer Engagement:** Offer suitable workout plans and additional services.
